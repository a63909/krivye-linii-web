# Gaming catalogue

The catalogue contains 24 named CS2 and Dota 2 items in four groups. It is a
made-to-order enquiry catalogue: no inventory, fixed price, immediate delivery,
commercial authorisation by Valve, or payment-provider approval is asserted.
Each request identifies the selected item. Availability, the actual specimen,
price in RUB, permitted transfer method and delivery time must be confirmed
before payment. Customer-facing links to order/refund terms remain visible.

Item identities were checked against Steam Community Market listings on
2026-11-03. The catalogue links to the relevant Steam item or search. Being
listed on Steam is not evidence of the operator holding inventory or approval
for every third-party commercial arrangement.
